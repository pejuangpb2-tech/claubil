# APA 7: English

## In-text citations

| Case | Parenthetical | Narrative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One author | (Smith, 2020) | Smith (2020) |
| Two authors | (Smith & Lee, 2020) | Smith and Lee (2020) |
| Three or more | (Smith et al., 2020), from the first citation | Smith et al. (2020) |
| Group author, first use | (World Health Organization [WHO], 2021) | World Health Organization (WHO, 2021) |
| Group author, later | (WHO, 2021) | WHO (2021) |
| No date | (Smith, n.d.) | Smith (n.d.) |
| Same author, same year | (Smith, 2020a, 2020b) | Smith (2020a) |
| Several works | (Adams, 2019; Brown & Chan, 2021), alphabetical, semicolons | |
| Secondary source | (Piaget, 1952, as cited in Lee, 2019); only Lee goes in the list | |
| Personal communication | (A. Rahman, personal communication, March 3, 2024); not in the list | |

- Direct quotation: add the location, (Smith, 2020, p. 15), (Smith, 2020, pp. 15–16), or for unpaged works (Smith, 2020, para. 4) or a section name.
- 40 words or more: a block quotation, indented 0.5 in, no quotation marks, the citation after the final full stop.
- Within one paragraph, after the first narrative citation of a work, later narrative mentions of it may leave out the year: "Smith (2020) found ... Smith also reported ...". Parenthetical citations always keep the year.
- Use "&" inside parentheses and in the reference list, "and" in running text.

## Reference list

- Title **References**, centred, bold, on a new page. Entries double-spaced with a 0.5 in hanging indent, sorted alphabetically by the first author's surname, letter by letter; "nothing precedes something" (Brown, J. before Browning, A.).
- List up to 20 authors. For 21 or more: the first 19, an ellipsis, then the last author.
- Initials with full stops and spaces: Lee, A. B.
- Titles of articles, chapters and web pages in sentence case; journal names in Title Case and italic with the volume italic.
- DOIs as `https://doi.org/...`; no "Retrieved from" unless the page changes and a retrieval date is needed. No publisher location for books.
- Same first author: single-author works first, then by year; same authors and year: add a, b, c by title.

### Patterns

**Journal article**
Ahmad, S., & Tan, M. L. (2021). Pronoun use in early primary writing. *Journal of Language Studies*, *15*(2), 45–60. https://doi.org/10.1234/jls.2021.015

**Book**
Hassan, N. (2019). *Teaching grammar through play* (2nd ed.). Penerbit Universiti.

**Edited book**
Lim, K. W. (Ed.). (2020). *Language education in Southeast Asia*. Routledge.

**Chapter in an edited book**
Omar, R. (2020). Bilingual classrooms. In K. W. Lim (Ed.), *Language education in Southeast Asia* (pp. 101–118). Routledge.

**Web page**
Ministry of Education Malaysia. (2023, May 2). *Primary school curriculum framework*. https://www.moe.gov.my/...

**Thesis or dissertation, from a database**
Zainal, A. (2022). *Pronoun acquisition among Year 4 pupils* [Master's thesis, Universiti Pendidikan Sultan Idris]. UPSI Digital Repository. https://...

**Unpublished thesis**
Zainal, A. (2022). *Pronoun acquisition among Year 4 pupils* [Unpublished master's thesis]. Universiti Pendidikan Sultan Idris.

**Conference paper in proceedings**
Yusof, H. (2021). Gamified grammar lessons. In *Proceedings of the International Conference on Education* (pp. 12–20). Publisher. https://doi.org/...

**Report by a group author**
UNESCO. (2022). *Global education monitoring report 2022*. https://...

These examples show the pattern only: their names, titles, volumes, pages and DOIs are illustrative and must never be used as real references.

## Paper format (student paper)

- Margins 1 in (2.54 cm) on all sides; double spacing throughout, including the reference list.
- Fonts: Times New Roman 12, Calibri 11, Arial 11, Georgia 11, Lucida Sans Unicode 10 or Computer Modern 10.
- First line of each paragraph indented 0.5 in; text left-aligned, ragged right.
- Page number top right on every page. A running head is only required for professional papers.
- Title page: paper title (bold, centred, upper half), author, affiliation (department, university), course code and name, instructor, due date.
- Abstract only when asked; usually up to 250 words, followed by keywords.

## Headings

| Level | Format |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | **Centred, Bold, Title Case** |
| 2 | **Flush Left, Bold, Title Case** |
| 3 | ***Flush Left, Bold Italic, Title Case*** |
| 4 | **Indented, Bold, Title Case, Ending With a Full Stop.** Text runs on. |
| 5 | ***Indented, Bold Italic, Title Case, Ending With a Full Stop.*** Text runs on. |

The introduction does not take the heading "Introduction"; the paper title at the top of the first page serves.

## Tables and figures

- Number in bold above: **Table 1** / **Figure 1**; the title on the next line in italic Title Case.
- Notes below the table or figure: *Note.* followed by the explanation and, if adapted, the source in APA form.
- Refer to every table and figure in the text by number before it appears.

## Language

- Formal, precise, concise; prefer active voice where it reads clearly. First person ("I", "we") is acceptable for the author's own actions.
- Numbers: words for zero to nine, numerals for 10 and above; numerals always for statistics, percentages, ages, times, dates and measurements.
- Bias-free language: specific, person-first or identity-first as the group prefers; singular "they" is accepted.
- Verb tense: past or present perfect for the literature review and method; past for results; present for discussion of implications.
