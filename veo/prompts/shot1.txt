In front of a traditional wooden Malay stilt house (rumah kampung) decorated with hanging woven ketupat and bamboo oil lamps (pelita), a Malay family wears matching emerald green baju raya: the father in baju melayu, black songkok and a maroon songket samping; the mother in baju kurung and a cream tudung.

Beside them, their little daughter in green baju kurung and a cream tudung does salam to her mother, and the mother hugs her tenderly.
