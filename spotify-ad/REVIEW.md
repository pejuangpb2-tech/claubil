# Review — Spotify spec ad

Checked against SPEC.md with `hyperframes check` (no errors) and `snapshot --review` plus extra frames at every seam.

## What holds
- One neon thread runs the film: spirals into the mark, leaves right, enters the phone and traces its edge,
  weaves under the covers, threads friends into the Jam queue, branches to the devices, underlines the end lockup.
- Transitions, each caused on screen: carry-across (thread), zoom through the album art, object becomes the
  world (art shrinks back into the front cover), horizontal whip, blur swap, vertical whip, colour flood,
  iris down to the mark's circle.
- The mark is the brand's own path (simple-icons), never redrawn. End card: mark + name, CTA, "made by fanad",
  "Spec ad · Not affiliated with Spotify", held to the end.
- All reading text at or above 28 px; lines held past their read floor.

## Known limits
- No music bed: no music source in this session (no HeyGen sign-in; model hosts blocked).
- English voiceover (Kokoro, voice Heart) in assets/voice/, one line per scene; effects ducked to 60%.
  Final mix loudness-normalised to about -15 LUFS, true peak -1.4 dBTP, no clipping. Measured, not listened to.
- The wordmark is typeset in Inter Display Black, not Spotify's own logotype file (not reachable here).
- The phone, playlists, lyrics, Wrapped numbers and Jam are illustrative, not captures of the real app.
